Privacy and videos

Children's data

For a child we keep only a first name or nickname, an avatar and a birth year. We do not ask for a child's photo, phone or email. There is no chat and no public profile. An adult can delete a profile and its progress at any time.

Videos

Lesson videos are free videos from their official YouTube channels (for example Alphablocks, British Council LearnEnglish Kids and BBC Learning English). We embed them with YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) and add no ad or analytics trackers to lesson pages. We do not download or re-upload any video. FamilyEnglish is not affiliated with these channels.

Phonics

Level 0 follows the widely used Jolly Phonics teaching order of the 42 letter sounds. The actions, notes, word lists and pictures in FamilyEnglish are our own. Jolly Phonics is a trademark of Jolly Learning Ltd.
